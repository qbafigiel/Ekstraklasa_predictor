  Ta sama metodologia co oficjalny baseline model_yellow_cards.py
  Poisson
  log(mu_home) = b_home + w_for*log(home_for_avg) + w_against*log(away_against_avg) + w_total*log(home_total_for_avg)
  log(mu_away) = b_away + w_for*log(away_for_avg) + w_against*log(home_against_avg) + w_total*log(away_total_for_avg)
  Challenger dodaje:
      + w_ref * ref_yc_log_ratio
  gdzie:
      ref_yc_log_ratio = log(ref_yc_shrunk_before + 0.5) - log(league_yc_avg_before + 0.5)
  Pelny rozklad sumy zapisany jako p_sum_0 ... p_sum_12, p_sum_13_plus

  Rolling window: 8 meczow
  Porownanie 1:1: baseline vs referee
